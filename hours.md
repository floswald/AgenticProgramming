# Teaching Hours Plan

## Academic hour conversion

An *ora accademica* (academic hour, "ah") = 45 min of teaching — the standard Italian
university convention. Note on sourcing: 1 CFU = 25 h of total student engagement is a
real, citable rule (D.M. 270/2004; UniTo Regolamento Didattico di Ateneo, Parte I —
Norme Comuni, Art. 10 comma 1, DR n. 6752/2024). The "45 min = 1 ah" conversion itself
is *not* in that ateneo-wide text — it's customary, confirmed e.g. in University of
Trieste's teaching-duties regulation (Art. 7), but not verified for UniTo/ESOMAS
specifically. Treat it as convention, not cited UniTo regulation, unless ESOMAS's own
regolamento didattico is checked.

```
ah = (clock minutes actually taught) / 45
```

A single-session meeting = 90 min = **2 ah**. A double-session meeting = 180 min (the
full slot) = **4 ah**.
